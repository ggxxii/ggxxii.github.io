I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Hongsheng Li at MMLab, CUHK. I am also a research intern at ACE Robotics.

I am currently interested in scaling robot learning with human data — turning large-scale egocentric human video into usable supervision for manipulation.

Before that, I worked on 3D human and garment generation with diffusion models and Gaussian Splatting during my M.S. at Shanghai University, and was a research intern at Tencent Youtu Lab with Junwei Zhu and Junshu Tang.
